Development and maintenance of Zirius ERP in a small company and development team, where responsibilities extended well beyond software development into architecture, product design, customer support, hardware, deployment and day-to-day operations. Zirius ERP is a Norwegian accounting and business management system covering accounting, orders, warehouse, invoicing, procurement and project management, with industry-specific modules and integrations.

  • Had technical responsibility for large parts of the product, working across architecture, technical leadership and hands-on development.
  • Designed and implemented ERP functionality for project management, warehouse, invoicing, document flow, accounting and fixed assets.
  • Built integrations with banks, Altinn, NETS, debt-collection providers, Brønnøysundregistrene, product registers and external ERP systems.
  • Designed and developed document handling and OCR-based flows for automatic processing of invoices and vouchers.
  • Developed native Android applications for time tracking, warehouse and timber workflows, including offline support.
  • Contributed to POS and payment solutions.
  • Led smaller development projects and worked closely with customers and domain specialists on requirements, design and implementation.

Technology

Java, MySQL, Swing, Tomcat, Android, REST, SOAP, XML, JSON, SQL, JasperReports, Subversion and Web Services.
